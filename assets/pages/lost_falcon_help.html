Shot down over the Syrian desert, you barely survived the ejection. Now, alone and hunted, you must cross hostile terrain, manage your dwindling strength, and avoid enemy patrols to reach safety.

Based on the excellent solitaire game “1972: The Lost Phantom”. Go look it up and play it!

Objective

How to Win

Reach the U.S. forces patrolling the edge of the map.

You score points for every hex you have mapped by the end of the game. If you win, you also gain a bonus equal to your remaining Proximity, Health, and Endurance.

How to Lose
  • Health = 0 – you die or bleed out.
  • Proximity = 0 – enemy forces capture you.
  • Instant death from certain Encounter results.

Core Stats

You track three main stats. Each starts at 6 and can never go above 6.

Health
Your physical condition. If Health reaches 0, you die. Many wounds and “6” results reduce Health.
Proximity
How close enemy militants are to you. If Proximity reaches 0, you are captured.
Endurance
Determines how many dice you can allocate each turn. Low Endurance means fewer choices and lower odds of success.

Turn Structure

Each turn is played in five phases, always in the same order.

Phase 1 – Encounter

Encounters can give you items, wounds, terrain changes, forced movement, or even a chance to win via rescue. Different encounters happen depending on how far you've moved. There is no Encounter phase on the very first turn.

Phase 2 – Dice Allocation

You receive a number of dice equal to your current Endurance. Allocate them to:

  • Move – leaving your current hex.
  • Stealth – keeping Proximity from dropping.
  • Rest – recovering Endurance.

Once placed, dice are locked until the end of the turn.

Phase 3 – Move

Roll your allocated Move dice and choose the highest. The terrain you are leaving determines the target number (e.g., it is easier to move out of scrub than hills).

  • Success – move into an adjacent hex.
  • Failure or no dice allocated – stay in the current hex.
  • Roll a 6 – success, but lose 1 Health.
  • Village – entering a Village triggers a special event.

If you successfully moved this turn, you may reroll one die in the Stealth phase.

Phase 4 – Stealth

Roll your allocated Stealth dice and choose the highest. Terrain affects how hard it is to hide (e.g., it is easier to hide in rough terrain than brush).

  • Success – Proximity does not change.
  • Failure or no dice allocated – lose 1 Proximity.
  • Roll a 6 – success, but lose 1 Health.

If you are in a different hex than you started the turn in, you may reroll one die after rolling.

Phase 5 – Rest

Roll your allocated Rest dice and choose the highest. Terrain modifies how easy it is to recover.

  • Success – gain 1 Endurance (up to a max of 6).
  • Failure or no dice allocated – lose 1 Endurance.
  • If Endurance would drop below 1, lose 1 Health instead.
  • Roll a 6 – success, but lose 1 Health.

You may choose to intentionally fail a roll to avoid taking a 6‑wound if your Health is low.

Afflictions

Dangerous encounters and certain rolls can give you Afflictions. You can only have each affliction once (except a Superficial Wound).

Inventory

Inventory items are found during Encounters. Each item can only be acquired once and used once. After use, it is considered spent or broken.

Survival Tips

Protect your Endurance.
More Endurance means more dice to allocate, which means better odds in every phase.
Health is precious.
Rolling 6s is powerful but costly. Only accept a 6‑wound when the payoff is worth it.
Use terrain smartly.
Hills are safer for Stealth; open terrain like scrub is risky but sometimes necessary.
Items change everything.
A Flare Gun, Binoculars, or Motorcycle can completely alter your route and odds of survival.
